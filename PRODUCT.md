# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, policy analysts, workforce planners, and labor-market specialists studying GCC employment data. They use Mihna to inspect market signals, compare cohorts, and ask grounded questions against the available job-posting corpus.

## Product Purpose

Mihna turns GCC job-market data into an explorable research workspace. The dashboard supports structured inspection, chat supports evidence-grounded inquiry through the existing RAG system, and analyze will support deeper comparative workflows in a future release.

## Brand Personality

Assured, rigorous, and clear. Mihna should feel like a modern research instrument: composed enough for policy work, direct enough for daily analysis, and recognizably connected to its editorial landing experience.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI assistant interfaces with neon gradients, emoji-led controls, or novelty chat styling.
- Legacy admin dashboards with crowded chrome, ornamental cards, and inconsistent navigation.
- Marketing layouts transplanted into product workflows.
- Visual changes that alter or obscure the existing RAG behavior.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the research task primary and the application chrome quiet.
2. Give dashboard, chat, and analyze independent URLs and clear page identities.
3. Extend Mihna's established identity through typography, color, and language without copying landing-page composition into the product.
4. Preserve behavioral contracts and data flows while replacing legacy presentation comprehensively.
5. Use familiar, accessible controls and explicit states so the interface remains trustworthy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard operability. Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve English and Arabic directionality, avoid color-only meaning, and keep controls usable across desktop and mobile viewports.
